Transforming Supply Chain Management with Trust and Technology

Transforming Supply Chain Management

Founded in 2020, Product Tracking System (PTS) emerged from a simple yet powerful vision: to create a world where consumers can trust the products they purchase, and businesses can protect their brands from counterfeit threats.

Our journey began when our founders witnessed firsthand the devastating impact of counterfeit products on both businesses and consumers. We realized that existing solutions were either too complex or insufficient to address the growing challenges in modern supply chains.

Today, PTS stands as a leading provider of comprehensive product tracking and authentication solutions, serving businesses across multiple industries and helping protect millions of products worldwide.

Our team working together to create innovative solutions

Our Mission

To empower businesses with innovative tracking solutions that eliminate counterfeit products, ensure supply chain transparency, and build consumer trust through technology that's both powerful and accessible.

Our Vision

We envision a future where every product can be authenticated instantly, supply chains are completely transparent, and counterfeit goods are eliminated from global markets.
